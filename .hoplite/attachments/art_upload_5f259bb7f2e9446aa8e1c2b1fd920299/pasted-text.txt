- Excellent whitespace and visual hierarchy
- Modern typography similar to Inter, SF Pro or Geist
- Minimal but highly polished
- Professional fintech/travel-tech aesthetic
- Desktop-first responsive design
- Avoid excessive gradients and visual clutter
- Use blue gradients only for important CTAs and active elements
