PART 1 — BTICKET CORE PRODUCT & HOMEPAGE DESIGN

Create a premium, world-class travel-tech startup website called “BTicket”.

BTicket is an automated travel booking platform for:
✈ Flights
🚆 Train tickets

The platform helps customers find tickets, monitor availability, receive notifications and request automated booking.

BRAND POSITIONING:

BTicket
“Your journey. We find the way.”

Core concept:
The customer tells BTicket where and when they want to travel. BTicket searches for suitable tickets and helps them secure the trip without requiring the customer to constantly check availability.

IMPORTANT:
This is a premium startup product design, not a generic travel agency website.

The design should look like a serious international technology company with a polished, trustworthy and modern identity.

==================================================
1. GLOBAL VISUAL IDENTITY
==================================================

Use a premium white-first visual system.

Main colors:
- White
- Soft blue
- Deep navy
- Electric blue
- Very subtle violet accents

Visual ratio:
Approximately 70% white,
20% light blue,
10% strong blue accents.

Style:
- Premium travel technology
- Modern SaaS
- Clean minimalism
- Elegant glassmorphism
- Large rounded cards
- Soft shadows
- Thin borders
- Excellent whitespace
- High-quality realistic travel photography
- Modern typography similar to Inter, SF Pro or Geist
- Subtle gradients
- Smooth micro-interactions
- Sophisticated iconography

Avoid:
- Cheap travel-agency aesthetics
- Excessive neon
- Dark cyberpunk visuals
- Overloaded interfaces
- Excessive gradients
- Generic stock-template appearance

The overall feeling should be:

TRUSTWORTHY + PREMIUM + FAST + SMART + GLOBAL

Use:
- Consistent spacing

- Consistent rounded corners
- Consistent blue accent system
- Premium cards
- Realistic travel photography
- Clean icons
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Smooth transitions

The result should look like a real venture-backed travel-tech startup homepage ready for an international market.

Do NOT make it look like a student project.

Prioritize premium branding, conversion, trust and clarity.
